
## Welcome.

This is where I publish both my *long-form* and *short form* writings. I might throw in a book review or two in between when I feel compelled to write a review on my readings. 

When I'm not writing here, I'm building [**Thumos**](https://thumospress.com/). A print first bi-monthly periodical focused on long-form essays. The usual categories include book reviews, social and political commentary, poems, criticism, and art. We intend to promote the tradition of the little magazine focused on long-form writing. The format might even supersede the contents themselves. We are open to [contributions](https://thumospress.com/write.html). 

The site is hand built in HTML and CSS.
